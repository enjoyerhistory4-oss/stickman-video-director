**Countdown clock at start:** Food: ~9 days · Water: running · Battery: 100% (plugged in)

**Standing rules for every scene**

- You are never shown: only **your two stick hands** (thin band on the **left wrist**) and **the phone** (cracked top-right corner, battery icon always visible).
- Camera = your eyes. No shots of your face, body, or reflection.
- Zombies are **grey** stickmen with slumped posture, one arm lower, **X eyes**. Everyone else is black.
- PG-13: danger is implied, not graphic.

## Continuity Notes (for later steps)

- Both choices lead into Episode 2 ("Inventory") without changing the story bible. The choice only colors Ep 2's first few seconds (A: you wake up on the floor by the window; B: Dev's whisper "just stay in, man" becomes the first chat message in Ep 2). The Slow One loses interest either way.
- World rules introduced here: **sound attracts them** (rule 1), **locked doors hold** (rule 3), **alerts are vague** (rule 9), and **the countdown HUD** (rule 12).
- Props on screen: phone, charger, deadbolt, chair, blinds, cupboard food (cans, crackers, rice), lamp, fridge.
- Mrs. Okafor's lit window is set up in Scene 5 but she isn't seen until Episode 3.
- Night + phone glow: the screen lighting up in Scene 7 sets up rule 4 (light at night) for later episodes, but isn't explained yet.
